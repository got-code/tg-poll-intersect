How grouping and filtering work

Group by — Each option in the poll selected under “Group by” gets its own card. Inside, see the participants and their choices in the other polls.

Filter by — Inside each card, count people for each option in the poll selected under “Filter by”. Hide options below the minimum, and hide the card if no option reaches it.

For example —

Set Group by to “Which date?” and Filter by to “What shall we discuss?”, with a minimum of 3 people. In the “16 November” card:

  • Books has 4 people — shown.
  • AI has 3 people — shown.
  • Science has 2 people — hidden.

Raise the minimum to 5: the “16 November” card disappears because no discussion option in that card has 5 people. Totals include implicit participants marked with ?.

Filter options — In the Filter by dropdown, choose Same as Group by to apply the minimum to each card’s total number of people. This option uses the poll selected under “Group by”. Choose a poll name to keep filtering that poll when “Group by” changes, or None to show every card and option.

Privacy

The app connects directly to Telegram. No application backend or analytics. It never votes or sends messages; it may read your chat list to resolve private group links.

“Remember login” encrypts your session and API keys with a separate password in this browser. Passwords and login codes are never saved. Reloading clears results and locks the saved login. “Sign out” removes it and attempts server logout; you can revoke sessions in Telegram → Settings → Devices. The session has full account permissions. Encryption protects the stored copy, not an unlocked page or compromised device.
